Private finance, without public exposure.

Your balances, trades, and credit history shouldn't be readable by anyone with a block explorer. CipherShield keeps them encrypted end to end, even while they're being computed on.

Onchain shouldn't mean visible to everyone.

Every wallet balance, every trade size, every credit history sits in the open today. Competitors watch your positions. Counterparties front-run your trades. Lenders profile you from a public address. CipherShield closes that gap.

Three products, one guarantee: your data stays yours.

Each is built on the same encrypted core, so the privacy is never bolted on after the fact.

01 — Risk

CipherRisk

Model exposure and liquidation thresholds across your DeFi positions without ever putting position sizes on public display.

02 — Payments

ShieldPay

Send and receive payments where the amount and counterparty stay between the two of you, not the entire chain.

03 — Credit

Private Credit

Build a portable, onchain credit score from your repayment history, computed without decrypting the underlying data.

About CipherShield

We think financial privacy shouldn't be the price of using a public blockchain.

CipherShield is built on Fhenix's fully homomorphic encryption infrastructure so that balances, trades, and credit history can stay encrypted even while smart contracts compute on them. Nothing about using a public chain requires broadcasting your financial life to it.

Privacy by default

Every product starts encrypted. Visibility is something you opt into, not out of.

No compromise on composability

Encrypted values still work inside DeFi — lending, payments, and credit all interoperate.

You hold the keys

Only you decide who can ever decrypt your data, including CipherShield itself.
